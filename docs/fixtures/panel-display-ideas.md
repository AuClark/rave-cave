# Display build ideas

Ideas for housing the four PH6 panels (each 192 × 96 mm, 32 × 16 px, 6 mm pitch, ~13–18 mm thick). Nothing here is decided yet.

## Preferred direction: tall external pillar

A standalone column, taller than a single panel cube. Options:

- **Frosted acrylic column** with the PH6s as a belt around it or as an inner cube
- **Clear acrylic** with light rods
- **Faceted mirror skin** over the panels
- **Hybrid**: PH6 panels plus WS2815 strips (same strip type as the main rig)

Open questions:
- Pillar height?
- Buy acrylic tube, or build a frame and skin it?

## Infinity mirror

Two-way acrylic at the front, solid mirror at the back, LEDs between them.

- Hollow square of all four panels: about **384 × 384 mm** outside, **192 × 192 mm** opening
- Needs roughly **400–450 mm square** cuts: one two-way, one solid mirror
